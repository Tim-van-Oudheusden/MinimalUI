Cloned apps (dual apps) now show with your other apps and in their category, marked Clone, instead of in the Work group (Android 15 and later). Groups and collections whose names differ only in case, like games and Games, are now one. Fixed a crash when an app was installed or removed just as the launcher reloaded, for example after a dark mode switch. Under the hood, settings storage was simplified; hidden and renamed apps, groups and favourites carry over.
